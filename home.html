hey there, welcome to my blog!

hello there everyone, i'm mwangi! thanks for stopping over to my little corner of the internet. this is just a place for me to put basically anything i want, think of it like all of my social media except it's one page that i coded on my own :D

it took a lot of time and learning to figure out how to make this blog page look and act like i wanted it to, so if you're viewing on anything other than a computer you're not seeing the full, awesome theme! i'd apprecite it so so much if you'd check out my site on a computer :D

my goal in creating this website was to make something nostalgic- something that reminded me of my own days on the early internet, and to get away from social media. i wanted to get that late 90s/ early 2000s vibe, apply my own aesthetic and interests, and hopefully fart out something good. and hey look, this is what i ended up with! not too bad if i do say so myself.

hot take (maybe not so hot): i think social media has been the downfall of overall creativity on the internet. not to mention engineered to be completely addicting. performative. and boring. you ever notice how every popular website looks pretty much the same? pretty lame if you ask me.

honestly, i'm just kinda sick and tired of being put into boxes (and being constaly tracked and served ads) on social media. i think its almost impossible to fully express yourself on places like instagram and tiktok. you can only control who you follow and what you post and nothing else, but with a website, everything is made by you, for you
